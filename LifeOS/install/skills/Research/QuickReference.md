# Research Quick Reference

## Research modes

| Trigger | Route | Method |
|---|---|---|
| “quick research”, one current fact | `Workflows/QuickResearch.md` | One focused discovery/extraction path plus source verification |
| ordinary “research this” | `Workflows/StandardResearch.md` | Several formulations, primary-source extraction, independent cross-check |
| “extensive research”, market or threat landscape | `Workflows/ExtensiveResearch.md` | Multi-angle coverage, bounded parallel workstreams, verifier pass |
| “deep investigation” | `Workflows/DeepInvestigation.md` | Persistent landscape/entity/profile/ledger artifacts and loop gate |
| “deep verified” or bulletproof claims | `Workflows/DeepVerifiedResearch.md` | Falsifiable claims, quotations, three verifier lenses, fail-closed vote |

Choose by evidence need, not page length. Do not launch a council of researchers for a single lookup.

## Hermes evidence ladder

1. `web_search` to discover current sources.
2. `web_extract` to inspect the actual page or PDF.
3. `browser_exec` for dynamic content or extraction failure.
4. A source-specific skill or tool when the source class requires it.
5. An optional paid scraper only after necessity, cost, and access boundaries are clear.

## Confidence

- `HIGH` — strong recoverable evidence plus independent confirmation.
- `MED` — credible single-source support or limited cross-check.
- `LOW` — weak, indirect, stale, or incomplete support.
- `CONFLICT` — credible sources disagree; preserve both sides.

## Completion check

- The requested question and depth are satisfied.
- Material claims have recoverable evidence.
- Broken, inaccessible, or mismatched sources are absent or clearly disclosed.
- Contradictions and uncertainty remain visible.
- Requested counts and artifacts are verified.
